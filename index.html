Greek TV Streamio

A Stremio / Nuvio addon that streams on-demand shows from Greek television stations. Catalog browsing, series, season and episode navigation, plus stream resolution.

Supported stations

Mega TV Skai Star Channel Alpha TV ERT ANT1

Install

Add this manifest URL to your Stremio / Nuvio addon list:

https://glogiotatidis.github.io/greektv-streamio/manifest.json

Or open manifest.json directly.

Endpoints

  • GET /manifest.json
  • GET /catalog/series/{id}.json
  • GET /meta/series/{id}.json
  • GET /stream/series/{id}.json
